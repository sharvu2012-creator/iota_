The Local Problem

Water stress in Punjab's agricultural belt

Punjab Agricultural Belt

Punjab's agricultural belt — once called the breadbasket of India — is under growing water stress. Groundwater is falling, temperatures are rising, and yields in some areas have plateaued or declined.

Research question: How have vegetation health and water coverage changed across Punjab's agricultural belt between 2023 and 2026?

Limitations of satellite data

Satellite imagery shows patterns. It rarely proves a cause on its own — read these before drawing conclusions.

Cloud cover

Clouds hide the ground, so cloudy scenes must be dropped or masked out.

Seasonal NDVI changes

Vegetation rises and falls with the seasons — compare like months, not like years.

Mixed pixels

One pixel can blend crops, roads and water, so small features are easy to miss.

“What”, not “why”

Images reveal that a change happened — not what caused it.

Sensor & date comparability

Different sensors and dates have different resolution and calibration — match them carefully.

Ground checks needed

Satellite findings should be verified on the ground before being treated as fact.
